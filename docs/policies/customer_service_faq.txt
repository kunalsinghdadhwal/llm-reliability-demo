Acme Corp Customer Service FAQ

Q: How do I contact Acme Corp customer support?
A: You can reach our customer support team by email at support@acmecorp.com, by phone at 1-800-ACME-HELP (1-800-226-3435), or through our live chat on acmecorp.com. Our support hours are Monday through Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM Eastern Time.

Q: What is your return policy?
A: All products purchased from Acme Corp may be returned within 30 days of the original purchase date for a full refund. The product must be in its original packaging and in unused condition. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after receiving the returned item.

Q: What does the warranty cover?
A: All electronics sold by Acme Corp carry a standard 1-year manufacturer warranty covering defects in materials and workmanship. Extended warranty coverage of up to 3 years is available for purchase within 30 days of the original purchase. Batteries, accessories, and consumable parts are covered for 90 days.

Q: How do I file a warranty claim?
A: Warranty claims must be submitted through our online portal at acmecorp.com/warranty with proof of purchase. Approved claims will be resolved through repair, replacement, or refund at Acme Corp's discretion.

Q: Do you ship internationally?
A: Yes, Acme Corp ships internationally to select countries. Rates are calculated at checkout based on destination and package weight. Customs duties and taxes are the responsibility of the recipient.

Q: How long does shipping take?
A: Orders are processed within 1-2 business days. Standard shipping typically takes 5-7 business days within the continental United States. Express shipping (2-3 business days) is available for $12.99, and overnight shipping is available for $24.99.

Q: Can I cancel my order?
A: Orders can be cancelled within 24 hours of placement if they have not yet been shipped. Once an order has shipped, it cannot be cancelled but may be returned under our return policy.

Q: Do you offer price matching?
A: Acme Corp does not currently offer price matching. Our pricing is set to provide the best value while maintaining product quality and customer service standards.

Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, and Acme Corp gift cards. All transactions are processed securely through our encrypted payment system.

Q: What if my item arrives damaged?
A: If your item arrives damaged or defective, contact our customer support team within 48 hours of delivery. We will arrange a free return shipping label and send a replacement or issue a full refund, including any shipping costs you paid.
